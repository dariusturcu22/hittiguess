Toasts are per-player and self-dismiss, pinned top right, always a colored circular icon plus a title and one line of context, never just a color with no words. Sidebar badges follow the same red-for-attention rule as a toast's error state, but drop to a plain dot once the exact count stops mattering, and disappear entirely rather than show a zero. Banners are the one notification type that speaks to everyone in the session at once, so they sit full width in the content column rather than floating in a corner.
