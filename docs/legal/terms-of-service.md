## The service

hittiguess lets players hear a song, guess when it was released, and place it on a shared timeline, either in person or online. Playback always happens on the real YouTube page or app; hittiguess never plays audio through a hidden or embedded player, and never uses an unofficial or reverse-engineered API for YouTube or any other external service.

## Content submitted by users

Submitting a song (title, artist, release year, a YouTube video ID, and related metadata) adds it to hittiguess's shared catalog. A submitted song stays part of that catalog even after the submitting account is deleted, it's treated as catalog data, not personal data tied permanently to one account. Users are responsible for only submitting content they have the right to reference, and for the accuracy of what they submit.
